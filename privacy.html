Privacy Policy — ShopPulse (Shopify Pixel, App & Catalog Inspector)

Last updated: November 24, 2026  |  Contact: ferrazmichel@gmail.com

1. Overview

ShopPulse is a read-only Chrome Extension designed to inspect publicly available storefront metadata on Shopify websites (such as theme information, marketing pixel IDs, installed app scripts, and public /products.json catalog feeds) strictly when initiated by the user.

2. Data Collection and Processing

  • No Personal Data Collected: ShopPulse does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personally identifiable information (PII), browsing history, authentication credentials, or payment details.
  • 100% Local Inspection: All storefront inspection, pixel detection, and CSV/JSON export generation happen locally inside your browser only when you click the extension icon.
  • Local Storage Only: User preferences and optional ShopPulse Pro license keys are stored locally on your device via chrome.storage.local and are never shared with third-party ad networks or analytics trackers.

3. Permissions Used

  • activeTab: Grants temporary access solely to the currently active browser tab when you explicitly click the extension icon.
  • scripting: Executes a short, self-contained read-only inspection script inside the active tab on demand to read public storefront metadata.
  • storage: Saves optional Pro license keys locally on your device.

4. Third-Party Services (Optional Pro License)

ShopPulse includes zero third-party analytics trackers and zero remote code execution. If you choose to purchase or activate an optional ShopPulse Pro license key, checkout and license key verification are handled via our payment provider (Polar.sh at api.polar.sh) solely when you click to open checkout or activate a license key.
